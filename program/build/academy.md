# The academy

Eight weeks, one master a week, built from the teachers the person named in the interview (Part F, question 37). If they named none, skip the academy and say so in the report. If they named fewer than eight, repeat the strongest ones with a second text rather than padding with teachers they did not choose.

`~/HQ/academy/curriculum.md`:

```markdown
# Academy · term starts YYYY-MM-DD

| Week | Master | Text | Framework to own | Homework (3) | Quiz day | Teach-back |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | 1. 2. 3. | Fri | Rest day, 3 min on camera |
| … | | | | | | |

## Scores
| Week | Homework /3 | Quiz /5 | Teach-back shipped | Notes |
```

How the agent runs it:
- **Homework** ties to the reading block: 20 pages a night from the week's text, plus three applied items that touch their real work (write the offer in the master's frame; run one conversation with the master's move; measure one thing the master says to measure).
- **Friday quiz:** five questions, a mix of recall and application to their live situation. Graded out of 5, honestly, in the terminal. A 3 or below repeats the concept next week before the next master.
- **Pop quiz:** one cumulative question in any conversation, any day, no warning. Wrong answer sends the concept back to homework.
- **Teach-back:** on the rest day they explain the week's framework in three minutes, on camera or out loud. The agent critiques honestly: what was clear, what was wrong, what they left out. A teach-back is also content if they publish.
- **Midterm, end of week 4:** twenty questions across the first four masters. **Final, end of week 8:** cumulative, plus an oral defense: the agent plays a skeptical buyer or counterpart and they hold the line with what they learned.
- **Grades are recorded immediately** in the Scores table, in their words where possible. No inflation. Weak scores repeat; the pretense of advancing is the enemy.

What to take from common teachers is in `~/HQ/knowledge/coaching-method.md`. Use it to pick the framework for each week, not to replace the person's own choices.
