# The island interview

Twelve questions. The island asks them on first open, but they are worth thinking through on paper first, or with a coach, or with an AI. Short answers are the right answers: a sentence or a number each.

Rule for every answer: it has to be something you can do on your worst day, not your best one. The run survives on the floor, not on the plan.

## The questions

1. **Name the island.** What is the island called? What is the run for, in one sentence? What do you return to every morning (your Source)?
2. **The run.** How many days? (90 is long enough to change something and short enough to feel every day.) What date is day 1?
3. **The frame of the day.** What time do you wake? What time are lights out?
4. **The one number.** What single number proves the run worked? What unit? Where is it today? Where must it be by the end?
5. **Life areas.** Which parts of your life is this run about? Pick up to eight (faith, body, work, money, mind, craft, home, people, or your own). For each, name the daily bar so that a yes/no or a number can answer it.
6. **The floor.** Three to five never-zero minimums. Small enough to do sick, tired or travelling.
7. **Work blocks.** When does the real work happen? Up to six blocks with a start, an end and a name.
8. **The rest day.** Which day does the island rest with you? What is that day for, so it stays a rest and not a drift?
9. **Two protected people.** Two relationships that do not get sacrificed to the run, and the fixed weekly touch for each.
10. **Three cuts.** Three things that are out for the length of the run.
11. **The miss rule and the sentence.** What happens the morning after a missed floor day? A cost you will pay, done first thing, not a punishment that breaks you. Then: the one sentence that says who you are during this run. Optionally, one teacher (a person or a book) you learn from each week.
12. **Rewards.** Ten milestones sit between your start and your goal. Decide the reward for at least the first three now.

## Running it with an AI as your coach

Paste this into any AI assistant, then answer its questions:

> You are my coach for a 90-day run. Ask me the twelve questions below one at a time. After each answer, push back once if it is vague, too ambitious for a worst day, or not measurable, then accept my revised answer and move on. Do not add questions. At the end, give me back my answers as a clean list I can type into the island, and tell me the one answer you think is the weakest and why.
>
> (paste the twelve questions here)

If you want the AI to change the island itself, give it `index.html` too and describe the change. The file is plain HTML and JavaScript; the questions live in the `Q` array, the tiers in `TIERS`, the building styles in `STYLE`.

## Reviewing the run

Once a week, on the rest day, look at the stones and answer three things:
- How many days were kept, and which letter was most common?
- Which building has not moved, and what is the smallest daily bar that would move it?
- Is the one number on pace? If not, what changes in the next seven days, not the next ninety?
